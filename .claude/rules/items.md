# Practice question rules

- A scenario of your own that ends in one clear question.
- Four options, A to D, all different; exactly one is right, named in answer.
- Each wrong option is wrong for a reason the lesson gives.
- No "all of the above" or "none of the above".
- why says in one line why the answer is right.
- quote is one sentence copied word for word from the lesson; section says where.
- A new scenario never repeats one already in bank/pending/ or bank/approved/.
