# What bread is made of

## Four ingredients

A basic loaf needs only flour, water, salt and yeast. Flour gives the structure, water wakes the flour's proteins and the yeast, salt controls the yeast and adds flavor, and yeast makes the gas that lifts the dough.

## Measuring by weight

Weigh the ingredients instead of using cups, because a cup of flour can weigh anywhere from 120 to 160 grams depending on how it is filled. Bakers write a recipe as percentages of the flour's weight, so 1,000 grams of flour with 700 grams of water is a dough at 70 percent hydration.

## Salt and yeast

Salt slows the yeast down, so too much salt gives a dense loaf and too little gives a fast, sticky dough that tastes flat. A common amount of salt is 2 percent of the flour's weight. Keep salt and dry yeast apart until the water is added, because direct contact can weaken the yeast.
