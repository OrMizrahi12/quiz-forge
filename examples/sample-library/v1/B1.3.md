# Proofing and baking

## Bulk proof

After kneading, the dough rises in one piece until it has grown by about half to double in size. A warm kitchen speeds this up and a cool one slows it down, so watch the dough rather than the clock.

## The poke test

Press a floured finger about one centimeter into the shaped dough. If the dent springs back at once, the dough needs more time; if it springs back slowly and leaves a small mark, it is ready to bake; if it does not spring back at all, it has over-proofed.

## Baking

Bake in a hot oven, usually between 220 and 250 degrees Celsius, with steam for the first 15 minutes so the crust stays soft while the loaf expands. A loaf is done when its inside reaches about 96 degrees Celsius, or when it sounds hollow when tapped underneath. Let it cool for at least an hour before slicing, because the inside is still setting.
