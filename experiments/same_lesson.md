Question 1
A marketing lead asks Claude to "write something about our new app" and gets a long, generic essay. A teammate who knows nothing about the launch reads the same request and has no idea what to write either. What does this suggest?
A) The model is too small for creative writing
B) The request fails the golden rule: add the audience, the goal and the format
C) The essay should be shortened by setting a lower max_tokens
D) Claude needs a role prompt that says it is a copywriter
Answer: B
Quote: "If they'd be confused, Claude will be too."
Why: A request a colleague with no context couldn't act on is too vague for Claude as well.
