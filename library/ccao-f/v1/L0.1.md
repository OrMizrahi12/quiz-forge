## Key terms

- **Blueprint / domain:** the official outline of what the exam tests. It has 7 content domains, and each one has a weight.
- **Multiple-response item:** a question with more than one correct option. The question tells you how many to pick.
- **Scaled score:** your result on a 100–1,000 scale. Every exam version then has the same pass bar of 720.
- **Criterion-referenced:** measured against a fixed standard, not against other candidates.
- **Minimally qualified candidate (MQC):** the exam's target person. It's a hands-on professional who completes common Claude workflow tasks independently.

## Exam facts in this lesson

- 60 items, multiple choice and multiple response. Each item says how many answers to select.
- 120 minutes, proctored, either online or at a test center. The exam is delivered by Pearson VUE.
- Scaled score from 100 to 1,000. The pass mark is 720. Scoring is criterion-referenced.
- Fee: 99 US dollars per attempt. Retake waits are 14, then 30, then 90 days, with up to 4 attempts in a rolling 12 months.
- The credential is valid for 12 months. On-time renewal is a free, non-proctored assessment.
- Domain weights:
  - Output Evaluation and Validation: 21%
  - Workflow Integration and Solution Design: 16%
  - Governance, Risk, and Responsible Use: 15%
  - Prompting and Task Execution: 14%
  - Product and Model Selection: 12%
  - Configuration and Knowledge Management: 12%
  - Troubleshooting and Optimization: 10%
- The weights are the approximate share of *scored* items. Per-domain question counts, such as "about 13" for Domain 2, are arithmetic estimates and are not stated in the guide.
- Eligibility, as of the date checked: certification is currently available to people at Claude Partner Network organizations who register with a company email.
